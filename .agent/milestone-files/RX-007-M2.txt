AUD-022: SkillExecutor and ProcessRunner subprocess execution is bounded and deadlock-free - stdout and stderr are drained CONCURRENTLY by channel-backed reader threads (a child flooding stderr while keeping stdout open cannot block the parent), a wall-clock deadline (SKILL_EXEC_TIMEOUT / PROCESS_EXEC_TIMEOUT, overridable via with_timeout) kills the process group on expiry (SkillExecutor) or the direct child (ProcessRunner, safe-only crate), the result is observable as SkillExecutionResult.timed_out / HarnessExitStatus::Timeout (never a fabricated success), and a bounded 2s receive grace prevents a pipe-inheriting grandchild from hanging the runner.
