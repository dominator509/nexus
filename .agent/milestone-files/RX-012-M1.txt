AUD-070: promotion authority is never reducible to a nonempty approval-reference string. evaluatePromotionGate now requires a full ManualPromotion record (parse fails closed without a signature) and verifies: real Ed25519 signature over the canonical approval payload against a pinned public key; approver in the authorized-approver policy (policy lookup); approved_at within the validity window (expiry; stale and future-dated denied); approver != requester (requester/approver separation); record binding to the exact canary ring and release; APPROVED_MANUAL_ONLY state with exact manual command. Hostile proofs: bare string, unauthorized approver, same requester/approver, expired, future-dated, tampered signature, wrong pinned key, wrong-ring binding all fail closed LOCKED.
