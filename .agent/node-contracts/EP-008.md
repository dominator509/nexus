# EP-008 NODE CONTRACT

Purpose: Implement OpenFGA, OPA, risk classes, short-lived grants, deterministic Action Gateway, verification, and receipts.

## Public interfaces
- RelationshipAuthorizer
- ContextPolicyEngine
- RiskClassifier
- ActionGateway
- CapabilityGrant
- ApprovalAssertion
- ActionReceipt
- VerificationPlan

## Acceptance obligations
- Models and agents cannot grant authority
- Every consequential action receives relationship and contextual policy decisions
- Short-lived grants are capability, target, actor, and expiry scoped
- Actions verify observable effects and produce receipts or fail closed

## Required specs
- .agent/specs/.agent/specs/SPEC-005-authentication-authorization-secrets-trust-and-multi-user-privacy.md
- .agent/specs/.agent/specs/SPEC-006-errors-reliability-idempotency-verification-and-action-safety.md

## Replacement and fallback
Use a small explicit OpenFGA model and OPA policy set; avoid a generalized policy language exposed to users.

## Reality rule
An interface is not operational merely because its type or adapter compiles. It must pass the owning node tests and any listed live-fire or certification proof.
